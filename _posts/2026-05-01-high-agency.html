In our modern lives, everything tends to move faster every day. This comes with both positive and negative consequences. On the positive side, things are becoming cheaper and less time-consuming. However, on the downside, we are becoming the product.

Companies compete for our attention, creating a sense of repetition that makes us feel like we are constantly missing out. This "cover" over our reality can dampen our spirits; we see so many people who have "already made it" that we feel stuck, relegated to watching others succeed from the sidelines.

Why do we think this way?

Much of it stems from our upbringing, but in the era of the smartphone, our devices have become the second most influential factor in our lives. We see people traveling the world or attending Tame Impala concerts on Instagram Reels, and it frustrates us.

In a crowd, the only people you notice are the ones screaming—not the silent majority.

We often hesitate to act because we fear being judged for not living up to the "Instagram standard." Fortunately, the reality is that almost no one will think about you for more than five seconds!

The Abyss Between Thinking and Making

Why are we so scared if we know no one cares? It is because we are the center of our own universe, and small things feel magnified to us. We lose ourselves in the "thinking stage," creating an abyss between thinking and making.

This is where High Agency comes in—it is the bridge that carries you across that abyss. Stop just believing: ask questions and move yourself one step forward.
