ENG

The main reason I created this blog is that I felt a lack of experience in these areas and wanted to kickstart things with a project. Honestly, the thought that no one will see this for years gives me a sense of comfort.

I’m currently a third-year Artificial Intelligence Engineering student. I love my field and want to pursue a career in it. Although there are times when I lose interest, I always find my way back, and this time, I want to shape my life around my responsibilities with more tangible results.

Outside of academics, I enjoy playing the guitar and improving my musical skills. I also feel a natural affinity for psychology; in fact, that was one of the reasons I chose this major: to understand the core of "how a computer thinks."
