I'm Yasin, a final-year Artificial Intelligence Engineering student at Hacettepe University, where the programme is taught in English. Since April 2026 I've been a research assistant working on computer vision for single-cell analysis: single-cell morphological profiling with Cellpose-based segmentation and graph representation learning.

I like building ML systems end to end. In practice that means spending as much time on leakage-free features and honest validation as on the model itself, and caring whether a model can run on local hardware. My interests are large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and AI security, alongside forecasting and computer vision.

Outside the lab I compete in algorithm events (16th of 130 at the inzva Algorithm Winter Camp 2026) and from 2023 to 2025 I was R&D Coordinator at the Hacettepe Artificial Intelligence Society.

Beyond the keyboard

I play the guitar and keep working on my music. I'm also drawn to psychology, which is part of why I chose this field in the first place: I wanted to understand how a computer "thinks". Lately I've been preparing for a marathon.
